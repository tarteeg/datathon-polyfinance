# Three portfolio simulations for the news agent

Choose an investor in **Settings → Investor persona**, then open **Sample portfolios** to preview that investor’s fictional allocation. Applying the sample replaces the account’s holdings. Open **News & Geopolitical Intelligence → Scan my portfolio** to investigate the latest 30 days.

On November 15, 2026 at 9:04 p.m. Toronto time, the StocksFeed database returned 2,300 canonical events for the rolling window. The counts below are distinct events tagged with at least one preset holding. They measure coverage. The agent separately checks business relevance, filings and publisher evidence before surfacing an event. Allocations are demo choices. The horizons and investor preferences come from the [challenge brief](../DATATHON-CHALLENGE-REPORT.md).

| Investor and sample | Tagged events | Case to investigate |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Amira · Chips, cloud and crypto | 53 | NVIDIA accounts for 25% of the sample. A Senate request for testimony about alleged chip diversions raises a question about export exposure. For her 15+ year horizon, ask what would establish a lasting sales constraint and whether the cloud position shares that risk. [Stored publisher story](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4672809-senators-demand-lutnick-testimony-over-alleged-nvidia-chip-diversions-to-china) |
| Mateo · Power transition and bonds | 30 | First Solar accounts for 15%. The South Carolina facility opening is evidence of capacity expansion. For an 8–12 year investor who cares about ESG, ask what utilization, emissions and workforce disclosures would establish the economic and environmental outcomes. [Stored publisher story](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4659849-first-solar-opens-first-phase-of-330m-south-carolina-facility) |
| Robert · Treasuries with a small AI position | 37 | PepsiCo accounts for 10%, across from a 65% Treasury ETF allocation and a 5% NVIDIA position. A restructuring gives Robert a reason to look for severance costs, recurring savings and cash-flow effects during his 3–5 year horizon. [Stored publisher story](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4669184-pepsico-reshapes-north-american-business-with-1000-job-reductions) |

For an indirect-exposure test, the November 15 report on AI supply-chain coordination carried zero ticker tags. Amira’s portfolio gives the agent a reason to investigate semiconductor and cloud businesses. An indirect finding still needs a concrete mechanism and issuer evidence. [Stored publisher story](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4672810-u-s-allies-agree-to-coordinate-ai-supply-chain-security)

For a side-by-side demo, keep one portfolio loaded and open the same PepsiCo story under each persona. Switch only **Investor persona**. Compare whether the explanation addresses diversification for Amira, workforce outcomes for Mateo and cash-flow resilience for Robert. The publisher excerpt, position and exposure provide a common basis for the comparison.
